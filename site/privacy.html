Privacy policy · Effective August 31, 2026

Your text stays yours.

ViraChin does not collect data. The app works locally and does not require an account or an internet connection.

Text and artwork

Persian text entered into ViraChin and transparent artwork generated by the app remain on your device. ViraChin does not transmit, log, analyze, or store this content.

Fonts and files

When you choose a local TTF or OTF font, ViraChin reads that user-selected file only to render artwork during the current session. It does not upload, copy, or retain the font.

Clipboard

ViraChin writes text or transparent PNG artwork to the system clipboard only after you press a copy button. It does not read or monitor unrelated clipboard content.

No tracking or third-party services

  • No analytics, advertising, tracking, or telemetry
  • No user accounts or identifiers
  • No backend service or cloud storage
  • No sale or sharing of personal data

Changes and contact

If ViraChin's data practices change, this policy and the relevant App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before release. Questions can be submitted through the public support tracker.
